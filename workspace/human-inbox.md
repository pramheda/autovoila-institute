# Human Attention Inbox

> Agents append decision-ready attention requests here.
> Routine work should not appear here.
> Preserve resolved requests and the human's response.

## Attention Types

### UPDATE

A material result changed the programme.

No immediate decision is required.

### REVIEW

A provisional interpretation or organisational change deserves human
inspection.

### DECISION

Several legitimate options remain and the choice depends on significance,
taste, responsibility, or direction.

### URGENT

Continuing without intervention risks invalid research, major waste,
hidden-data exposure, safety failure, or another hard-to-reverse error.

## Request Template

Use this structure when it helps:

- type: `UPDATE`, `REVIEW`, `DECISION`, or `URGENT`;
- source: branch, confirmation, artifact, or file path;
- what changed;
- why it matters;
- why agents cannot resolve it adequately;
- live options;
- recommendation and dissent;
- evidence links;
- default action if the human does not respond;
- deadline, if any;
- reversibility.

Do not force a rigid format if a short memo communicates better.

## Open Requests

None.

## Resolved Requests

Record:

- human response;
- date;
- action taken;
- and any later reassessment.
